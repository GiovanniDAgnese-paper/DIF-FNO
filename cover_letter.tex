\vspace{0.3cm}
\noindent \textbf{Core Scientific Contributions:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Novel Operator Architecture:} DIF-FNO overcomes the grid-restricted limitations of standard Fourier Neural Operators via learnable implicit diffeomorphic coordinate transformations.
    \item \textbf{Jacobian Regularization:} Metric distortion penalty based on the Jacobian determinant, ensuring smooth mapping to a canonical torus while preventing high-frequency Gibbs boundary artifacts.
    \item \textbf{Empirical Performance:} On complex non-convex star domains, DIF-FNO achieves a Relative $L^2$ error of $13.51\%$ and Sobolev $H^1$ error of $13.51\%$ with a compact footprint of $0.90\text{M}$ parameters.
\end{itemize}
